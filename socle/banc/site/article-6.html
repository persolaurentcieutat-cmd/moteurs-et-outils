Cruise season opens with three additional port calls

Publié le 2026-09-28 par le Service de l'information

The port authority has confirmed three additional cruise calls for the coming season, bringing the total to forty-one scheduled arrivals between November and April. Local operators expect roughly twelve thousand additional day visitors. Shore excursion providers have been asked to register their capacity before the end of October.
